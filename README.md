# KarmaCards Support

For help with **Karmacards - The Card Game**, contact
[jonathanfuerst2003@gmail.com](mailto:jonathanfuerst2003@gmail.com).

## Sign-in and game progress

Use the same sign-in method and account you originally used for the game.
Choosing another provider can open a different game account. If progress seems
missing, contact support before deleting your account or making another purchase.
Include your app version, device model, and a description of the problem.
Never send your password, verification codes, or full payment details.

## Purchases and coins

If a purchase has not appeared, check your connection and reopen the game using
the same game account. Contact support with the coin pack, purchase date, and
Apple transaction/order reference. Do not purchase the same pack again merely
to resolve a missing credit. Coins are consumable items: Restore Purchases is
not a way to restore coins that have already been spent.

For Apple billing or refund requests, use
[Apple's Report a Problem](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/).

## Rewarded videos

Ad availability varies. A reward is issued only when the ad provider confirms
completion. If no video is available, try again later. Report unexpected reward
behaviour with the approximate time, app version, and device model.

## Report or block a player

Open the player's entry in the friends list and choose **Report** or **Block**.
You can also report inappropriate names, profile images, or behaviour directly
to the support email above. Include the player name/ID if available, a description,
and the approximate date/time. Screenshots can help; remove unrelated personal
information. In versions offering **Copy Report**, paste the copied details into
an email or webmail, add the reason, and send it. Opening or copying a report does
not send it automatically.

## Delete your game account

Open the in-game **Settings**, choose **Delete Account**, and confirm only if you
want to permanently delete your game account and associated progress/profile data.
Deletion cannot be undone. If you cannot access the app, contact support for help.
Do not send your password.

## Privacy

For advertising consent choices, use **Privacy Options** in Settings when available.
For data-rights requests, contact the email above. Read our
[Privacy Policy](https://joni1544.github.io/karmacards-privacy-policy/).
